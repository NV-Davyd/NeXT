## About

NeXT is a mod for **CarX Drift Racing Online 2** that adds a driving HUD, player inspection and quality-of-life tools. The project is in active development.

Press **F10** in game to open the NeXT menu (you can change this key in **Settings**). Drag the menu by its top strip to move it.

## Features

- **HUD**: tyre temperature and wear, gear, speed, RPM, boost, pedals and a drift gauge that shows your drift angle, front wheels and stick together, as an arch under the speedometer or a small car seen from above. Move, resize or hide every widget, and hide the game's drift points and trick popups, track map, damage icons, pedals and steering, tyre panel and key hints. The track map and the game's speedometer, boost gauge and pedals can be moved and resized too. When you spectate, the HUD shows the car you're watching.
- **Player inspection**: see a nearby player's tyre sizes, rear pressure, horsepower and weight.
- **Fresh tyres**: swap to new tyres below 20 km/h.
- **Saved position**: save a spot on each map and return to it, even after leaving the map or the game. In a room, your car passes through other cars for a few seconds after you return.
- **Dynostand**: use the dyno while parked in a multiplayer room.
- **QoL fixes**: faster joining and leaving, for busy rooms, faster spectating, faster menus while driving, with a much shorter freeze when you switch between driving and the room screen, accurate car positions, which draws other players' cars where they are now instead of a frame behind, skip parts checkout, which buys and installs a Workshop part as soon as you select it, and handbrake lock, which keeps the handbrake on after you press it at a standstill until you press it again or drive off. Each has its own switch: handbrake lock under **Controls**, skip parts checkout under **Pit stop** and the rest under **Room fixes**. Skip parts checkout and handbrake lock start off.
- **Change car**: while stopped, pick another car from your car park. In a room, NeXT rejoins the same room with it so other players see it. Not available during scored runs.
- **Tune transfer**: Share → Export in the garage saves the car's parts and dynostand values with the file. Import with Upgrades ticked installs the exact parts, engine swap and tyres included, and buys the ones you don't own at the Workshop price. Suspension and brakes moving between a McPherson and a double-wishbone car become that car's own parts of the same stage. Tuning sets the values on whatever parts the car has.
- **Name and tag**: add a tag before or after your Steam name and colour both, with gradients or individual letter colours and saved looks. Your changes show after you next join a room.
- **Livery colours**: change every sticker of one colour at once in the livery editor, keeping each sticker's transparency. Keep the result with the game's Apply action.
- **NeXT players**: a mark beside the name and in the room list shows who runs NeXT. A red glow behind it means that player sees the other cars a frame behind.
- **Custom maps**: drive DRO1 Blender maps in solo free ride or rooms. Add a map folder or drag a map onto the game, then choose your own map or follow the room's. Next spawn moves you between spawn points.
- **Tyre sync**: other NeXT players' cars use their actual tyre wear and temperatures in your room, including while spectating.
- **Fly mode**: move your car freely to set up a position. In a room, other players can't see your car or its map icon while you fly, and collisions with other cars stay off until you land. The key left of **1** starts and stops it; aim with the mouse, move with **WASD**, rise with **E** or **Space**, sink with **Q** or **Ctrl**, hold **Shift** to go faster and **Alt** to go slower.
- **Graphics**: change time of day, sun, season, weather and picture settings, with up to five saved looks. Changes affect your own view; street lamps follow the session's time of day.

Features sit in three tabs at the top of the menu: **Driving**, **Room** and **Garage**. Use a feature's page for its switches, settings and keys. Related features share a page, one line or card each: **Pit stop** (Fresh tyres, Change car, Dynostand, Tune transfer and Skip parts checkout), **Controls** and **Room fixes** (the QoL fixes). Click your name to open **Account** and **Settings**, where the menu key, menu size and a button that centres the menu are. The coloured dot next to your name changes the menu's colour. Right-click a colour in Name and tag or Livery colours to copy or paste it.

## Installation

1. Close CarX and exit Steam (**Steam → Exit**).
2. Download and run the **NeXT Setup** installer from the [latest release](https://github.com/NV-Davyd/NeXT/releases/latest).
3. Start CarX with Steam's **Play** button and press **F10** to sign in through Steam.

Every approved player receives the public features above. Updates are checked every time you press Play; choose **Update** to install.

## Help

- After a CarX update, NeXT is disabled until a compatible release is out. You can keep playing without it in the meantime.
- If CarX crashes, the next **Play** asks whether to send the logs from that session. Nothing is sent unless you choose to.
- Report bugs and suggestions on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Zrqg7GyrB4).
